r/AskTrumpSupporters · reply-interaction network

Kernighan–Lin bisection of a political Q&A forum

Each node is a commenter; each edge is a direct-reply exchange. The from-scratch Kernighan–Lin implementation below splits the network into two equal halves that minimize total cross-group reply weight — then that split is checked against users’ own Trump Supporter / Nonsupporter flair.
Color people by "The algorithm's groups" is the two-way split Kernighan–Lin computed purely from reply patterns. "What they actually said" is each person's own Reddit flair. Switching between the two shows how well the algorithm's guess matches reality.

Where it matched reality Compares the algorithm's two groups to what people actually said about themselves. The green boxes are where they agree; the red boxes are where the algorithm got it wrong.

How the algorithm got there The algorithm starts from a random 50/50 split, then repeatedly swaps two people between groups whenever that reduces the connections left between groups. It tried this 10 times from different random starting points to make sure the result wasn't just a lucky guess.

Compared to other methods "Cut" counts the connections left between the two groups — lower means a cleaner split. "Modularity" is a general score for how well-defined the groups are — higher is better, and it still applies to methods that don't force exactly two groups.
